MUTANTMERGE

Merge goofy mutants into stronger ones, then send your team into automatic battles. Prototype build: tell us what feels fun and what feels off.

  1. 1Drag a creature onto another with the same level number to merge.
  2. 2Put your best mutants in the blue TEAM row.
  3. 3Tap BATTLE!, check the matchup, then FIGHT!
  4. 4CLAIM coins, buy Blobs and upgrades, merge again.

Works best on a phone in portrait. Progress is saved in this browser only. Coins are pretend money; there are no ads or purchases.
